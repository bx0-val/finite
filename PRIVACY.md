# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

Finite is a browser extension that counts the posts you scroll past on X. It is designed to collect as little as possible and to keep all of it on your device.

## What Finite stores

Everything is kept in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

- **Daily totals:** posts seen, ads, posts with media, words, scroll distance, active time, likes and reposts, each also split by hour.
- **Account handles:** the @handles of accounts whose posts you saw, used only for the "Accounts" count (capped at 2,000 per day).
- **Post ids for today and yesterday:** used to avoid counting the same post twice, then deleted automatically.

Finite **does not store the text, images or links of any post**, and it doesn't record your own posts or messages.

## What Finite sends

Nothing. Finite makes no network requests, has no servers and contains no analytics, telemetry or third-party code that phones home.

## Your control

- **Export CSV** in the dashboard downloads your daily totals.
- **Reset** in the dashboard deletes everything Finite has stored.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes its storage.
