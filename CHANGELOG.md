# Changelog

All notable changes to Finite are documented here. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-04

First public release.

### Added
- Counts posts scrolled past on X (at least half on screen, de-duplicated per day), across all open tabs.
- Tracks scroll distance, active time on X, words passed, ads, photos & video, likes, reposts and unique accounts.
- Dashboard with Today, Yesterday, 7-day, 30-day, Year and All-time ranges, charted by hour of day, day, week or month.
- Perspective comparisons: landmarks for distance, books for words, posts per minute, "1 like in N posts".
- CSV export and one-click reset.
- Light and dark themes.
- Chrome (MV3) and Firefox (115+) builds.
